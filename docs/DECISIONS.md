# Design decisions

Wherever the spec left a choice open, the simplest defensible option was taken. Each one is
recorded here with its reason.

| # | Date | Decision | Why |

| 18 | 2026-10-01 | Walmart coordinates from OpenStreetMap features whose `ref` is the store number | Verified on both smoke-test stores; ~3,900 Walmart features carry `ref` |
| 19 | 2026-10-01 | Hill estimator: report k = 5% of n as primary, with the stability-rule k (on k ≤ 15% n) and k = 10% as sensitivity | Simulation: on \|t₃\| data the stability rule over-reaches into the body (95% CI coverage 0.22–0.78) while k = 5% covers 0.84–0.92; all are fine on exact Pareto data |
| 20 | 2026-10-01 | GPD threshold at the 90% quantile of \|d\|, reported on the shape scale ξ | Comparable across chains; ξ ≤ 0 (no power tail) is possible and must not be forced into an α |

| 38 | 2026-10-02 | Module B rebuilt as a **two-part (observed hurdle) model**: part 1, is the price exactly the chain's list (modal) price, a logistic GLMM with store effects; part 2, if not, log(price/list) is Student-t with a store-level mean (non-centred store effects; + HSGP spatial field in M2). | 42 | 2026-10-03 | **Local session stash** (`collectors/session_stash.py`, `data/sessions/`, gitignored): one stored session per chain **per network (public IP)**, restored at the start of each run; saved after the first successful request. | 43 | 2026-10-03 | **One spare session per chain per network; one switch per run.** On a challenge (explicit or a 6-failure streak), the runner switches once to this network's spare (made in advance through the normal session start, never during a cooldown). No third session, no proxies, no session created after a refusal. Applies to Target, Costco and ALDI (sessions swappable in place); Walmart (per-store browser scoping) and Meijer/Kroger (no cookie session) still stop at the first block. | 44 | 2026-10-03 | **Simple session policy** (supersedes #42–43): each run starts with a **fresh** session and a fresh **spare**, both made through the collectors' normal session start before anything is refused. | 46 | 2026-10-03 | **What counts as a block:** only a FRESH session refused on its **first** request.
